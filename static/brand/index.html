Two tight lines, “pulse” in bold teal. Compact block that works on a square (social avatar, report cover), less suited to a slim navigation bar.

Usage (option A)

  • “toki” weight 500, “pulse” weight 800, letter-spacing −4.5%.
  • Dot: circle 20% of the font size, teal #14B8A6 (#5FD3C5 on dark).
  • Text colour: ink #1F2328 on light, #F0EFEC on dark. Never stretch or recolour the letters.
  • Clear space: the height of the “k” on every side. Minimum width: 90 px on screen.
  • Favicon / app icon: the letter “t” with the pulse dot, typographic as well.
